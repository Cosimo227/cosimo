    Selected work — 03

    Simulation · Digital twin

    Modelling a fulfilment centre before changing it

    CEVA Logistics worked with Simul8 to build a simulation-powered digital twin for a fashion retailer's fulfilment centre in Brandenburg, linked to real-time data from the centre's systems. Planners could test staffing and capacity decisions — how many people per shift, at what utilisation — against order-processing time, throughput and resource use, instead of experimenting on the live operation. The model runs continuous “what if” analysis over thousands of permutations.

    Average saving: 200 working hours a week. Weekly capacity: +2%. I'm quoted in the published case study.

    “The project amplified the warehouse planners’ processes, enabling them to allocate resources very precisely to the different stations. This allowed our customer to save working hours and invest in that free capacity in other areas and reduce costs.”
